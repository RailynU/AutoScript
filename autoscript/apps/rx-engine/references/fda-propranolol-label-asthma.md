## Contraindications and bronchospasm warning

Propranolol is contraindicated in patients with bronchial asthma, as well as in cardiogenic shock, sinus bradycardia and greater than first-degree block, and overt cardiac failure. Warnings note that beta-blockers, especially non-selective agents such as propranolol, can block the bronchodilating effect of endogenous and exogenous catecholamines and precipitate bronchospasm; in general, patients with bronchospastic lung disease should not receive beta-blockers, and if one is necessary a cardioselective agent at the lowest effective dose is preferred.

## Relevance to pharmacist review

Relevance to pharmacist review: propranolol (including for migraine, tremor or anxiety) in a patient whose profile lists asthma matches a labeled contraindication.
