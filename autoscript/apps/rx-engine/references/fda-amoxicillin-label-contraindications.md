## Section 4 Contraindications

Section 4 (Contraindications): amoxicillin is contraindicated in patients who have experienced a serious hypersensitivity reaction (for example anaphylaxis or Stevens-Johnson syndrome) to amoxicillin or to other beta-lactam antibacterial drugs such as penicillins and cephalosporins.

## Section 5.1 Anaphylactic reactions

Section 5.1 (Anaphylactic reactions): serious and occasionally fatal hypersensitivity (anaphylactic) reactions have been reported in patients receiving penicillin therapy. These reactions are more likely to occur in individuals with a history of penicillin hypersensitivity and/or a history of sensitivity to multiple allergens. Before starting amoxicillin, careful inquiry should be made concerning previous hypersensitivity reactions to penicillins, cephalosporins or other allergens. If an allergic reaction occurs, amoxicillin should be discontinued and appropriate therapy instituted.

## Relevance to pharmacist review

Relevance to pharmacist review: a documented penicillin allergy does not by itself tell the pharmacist whether the prior reaction was serious (contraindication) or low-risk (may be eligible for evaluation or challenge). The nature, timing and severity of the prior reaction must be clarified with the patient and prescriber.
