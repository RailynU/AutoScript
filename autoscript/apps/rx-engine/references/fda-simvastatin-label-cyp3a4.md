## Contraindicated inhibitors and dose caps

Because of the risk of myopathy and rhabdomyolysis, simvastatin is contraindicated with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors including itraconazole, ketoconazole, posaconazole, erythromycin, clarithromycin, telithromycin, HIV protease inhibitors, nefazodone, and also with gemfibrozil, cyclosporine and danazol. Dose caps apply with amiodarone, verapamil, diltiazem (10 mg per day) and with amlodipine and ranolazine (20 mg per day). The 80 mg dose is restricted to patients already tolerating it for 12 months or more.

## Relevance to pharmacist review

Relevance to pharmacist review: a short course of clarithromycin or erythromycin in a simvastatin patient is a contraindicated combination; options include holding the statin for the antibiotic course or choosing a different antibiotic.
