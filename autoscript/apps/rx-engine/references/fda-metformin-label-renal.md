## eGFR thresholds

FDA replaced serum-creatinine thresholds with eGFR-based guidance: metformin is contraindicated in patients with an eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73 m2; starting metformin in patients with eGFR between 30 and 45 is not recommended; eGFR should be obtained before starting and at least annually thereafter (more often in patients at risk of renal impairment, such as older adults); if eGFR later falls below 45, the benefits and risks of continuing should be assessed, and metformin should be stopped if eGFR falls below 30. Metformin should be discontinued at the time of, or before, an iodinated contrast imaging procedure in patients with eGFR between 30 and 60 and reassessed 48 hours after.

## Relevance to pharmacist review

Relevance to pharmacist review: a metformin prescription for a patient with advanced CKD on the profile requires confirming the current eGFR with the prescriber.
