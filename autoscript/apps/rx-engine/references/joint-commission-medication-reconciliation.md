## NPSG.03.06.01 medication reconciliation

NPSG.03.06.01 requires organizations to obtain information on the medications the patient is currently taking, compare that information with the medications ordered, resolve discrepancies, and provide the patient with written information about the medications they should be taking. It is framed around accurate, complete medication information as a prerequisite for safe prescribing and dispensing.

## ISMP allergy documentation

ISMP has repeatedly recommended that allergy information be obtained and documented for every patient before medications are dispensed, that the documentation include the nature of the reaction (not only the allergen), that "no known drug allergies" be recorded explicitly rather than leaving the field blank, and that pharmacy systems be configured so allergy fields cannot be bypassed. Unverified or missing allergy and medication history is a recognized contributor to preventable adverse drug events.

## Relevance to pharmacist review

Relevance to pharmacist review: when a prescription arrives for a patient with no linked profile, an empty allergy history, or a history recorded as "unverified", the allergy and interaction screening that a pharmacist relies on has not actually been performed. The system reports this as an explicit gap rather than as a clean result.
