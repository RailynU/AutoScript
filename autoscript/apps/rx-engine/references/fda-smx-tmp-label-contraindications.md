## Contraindications

Contraindications include known hypersensitivity to trimethoprim or sulfonamides, history of drug-induced immune thrombocytopenia with trimethoprim or sulfonamides, documented megaloblastic anemia due to folate deficiency, pregnancy at term and nursing, infants under 2 months, and marked hepatic damage or severe renal insufficiency when renal function cannot be monitored.

## Warnings: severe reactions and renal dosing

Warnings: fatalities associated with sulfonamides, although rare, have occurred from severe reactions including Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis, fulminant hepatic necrosis, agranulocytosis, aplastic anemia and other blood dyscrasias. The drug should be discontinued at the first appearance of skin rash. Dose reduction is advised with creatinine clearance 15 to 30 mL/min.

## Drug interactions: warfarin

Drug interactions: sulfamethoxazole-trimethoprim may prolong the prothrombin time in patients receiving warfarin; the label advises reassessing coagulation time when the two are co-administered.

## Relevance to pharmacist review

Relevance to pharmacist review: a documented sulfa allergy, significant renal impairment, or concurrent warfarin are each reasons to contact the prescriber before dispensing.
