## 325 mg per unit limit and boxed warning

FDA asked manufacturers to limit the strength of acetaminophen in prescription combination products (for example with opioids such as oxycodone and hydrocodone) to 325 mg per tablet or capsule, and required a boxed warning about the potential for severe liver injury and a warning about allergic reactions.

## Maximum daily dose and liver injury

The communication notes that severe liver injury has been associated with acetaminophen use exceeding the maximum daily dose of 4,000 mg, with taking more than one acetaminophen-containing product at the same time, and with alcohol use. Over-the-counter acetaminophen products carry similar maximum-dose directions. Many clinicians apply a lower ceiling (for example 3,000 mg per day) for older adults, people with hepatic disease, or regular alcohol users; that lower ceiling is clinical practice rather than part of this communication.

## Relevance to pharmacist review

Relevance to pharmacist review: calculate the total daily acetaminophen dose across all prescription and over-the-counter products before dispensing a combination analgesic.
