## 21 CFR 1306.05(a) required elements

Under 21 CFR 1306.05(a), all prescriptions for controlled substances must be dated as of, and signed on, the day issued and must bear the full name and address of the patient; the drug name, strength, dosage form, quantity prescribed and directions for use; and the name, address and DEA registration number of the practitioner. A prescription must be written in ink, indelible pencil or typewriter (or transmitted electronically under Part 1311) and manually signed by the practitioner, who is responsible for ensuring it conforms in all essential respects to law and regulation. A corresponding responsibility rests with the pharmacist who fills the prescription.

## State analogues for non-controlled prescriptions

This federal rule applies to controlled substances. State pharmacy practice acts and boards of pharmacy impose analogous completeness requirements (patient identification, drug, strength, quantity, directions, prescriber identification and date) on non-controlled prescriptions, and pharmacists are generally expected to clarify missing or ambiguous elements with the prescriber before dispensing.

## Relevance to pharmacist review

Relevance to pharmacist review: a medication line with no strength, directions or quantity, or a document with an unreadable prescriber or date, cannot be filled as written and needs prescriber clarification.
