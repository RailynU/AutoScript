# Where the curated eval labels come from

Checked 2026-10-24 against public sources. Machine-readable copy: `apps/rx-engine/src/eval/validation.ts` (typed, exhaustive over the curated case ids; each case in `cases.ts` carries its entry as `validation`, and `report.md` prints a "Label sources" row per curated case).

Verdicts: **CONFIRMED** = the source states the risk or requirement and is consistent with our severity and therapy-context expectation. **PARTIAL** = the source supports the finding but not our severity, wording or evidence expectation (what differs is stated). **DISPUTED** = the source contradicts an expectation. **NOT_FOUND** = no primary source could be opened.

First pass (2026-10-24, morning): 11 curated cases: 4 CONFIRMED, 6 PARTIAL, 1 DISPUTED, 0 NOT_FOUND; rule-level checks: 2 CONFIRMED, 1 DISPUTED. The check found three errors of ours, and the fixes were applied the same day (the "corrected" notes below say what changed and what gap remains): the Beers 2023 NSAID criterion's short-term clause was missing from the rules and the paraphrase; the Beers paraphrase carried an ACE inhibitor + NSAID "triple whammy" sentence that is not in the source; the allergy rule graded every non-severe direct match HIGH.

Totals after the corrections: 11 curated cases: 7 CONFIRMED, 4 PARTIAL, 0 DISPUTED, 0 NOT_FOUND. Rule-level checks: 3 CONFIRMED.

## Sources that could not be opened

- fda.gov Drug Safety Communication pages answered HTTP 404 to every non-browser request (direct, legacy `ucm` URLs, `media/download`); the Internet Archive copies above were read instead and are the URLs cited.
- www.ecfr.gov's HTML page redirected to a bot-check; the section text was read through the eCFR versioner API (XML as of 2026-10-01) and the human URL is cited.
- jointcommission.org (403) and the Wiley full text of Beers 2023 (403 behind Cloudflare): the Joint Commission 2026 NPSG chapter PDF on digitalassets.jointcommission.org and the PubMed Central copy of Beers 2023 (PMC12478568) were read instead.
- jacionline.org (403): the AAAAI-hosted pre-proof PDF of the 2022 practice parameter was read instead.
- cdc.gov penicillin-allergy pages and CDC STACKS (403/404 at every URL tried, no archive copy): not opened, not cited.
- A specific ISMP (US) publication on allergy documentation was not located; nothing from ISMP is cited.

## What this does and does not prove

It proves that each curated expectation was compared with current published labeling, the 2023 AGS Beers Criteria, the 2022 AAAAI/ACAAI practice parameter, 21 CFR 1306.05 and the 2026 Joint Commission NPSG chapter, and that where a source does not back an expectation the gap is written down (two PRN warfarin cases and the scheduled short course versus the 2023 Beers short-term clause; the Beers DDI-table citation on the ACE-inhibitor case; the HIGH severity on a remote benign penicillin reaction; the allergy-documentation requirement with no opened primary source). It does not replace pharmacist review: a confirmed label means the expectation matches the literature, not that the system's output for a real patient is correct or complete. It does not cover the 30 generated cases, whose expectations are derived from the generator's injected errors and were not checked against any external source. Severity levels are the system's own scale; no label grades severity, and the Beers "Avoid / Moderate / Strong" ratings are the only external grading used for comparison.
